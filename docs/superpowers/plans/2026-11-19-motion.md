# Motion and Transitions Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give the Overview hero and project cards quiet ambient life, and make tab switches, the assistant console, hovers and the case-study disclosure move smoothly with one shared rhythm.

**Architecture:**
- **CSS** does everything ambient (hero glow, grid drift, headline shimmer, card border light and float) and every hover. Shared timing lives in CSS custom properties.
- **Motion for React** (`motion` package, imported from `motion/react`) does things that glide or leave: the nav highlight, the console sliding in and out, the panel crossfade, the fit-row stagger and the disclosure unfold.
- **A small pure module** decides when ambient effects may run. A hook applies it, pausing them off screen or in a hidden tab.

**Tech Stack:** React 18.3, Vite 5, `motion` (Motion for React), CSS, `node:test`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-motion-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Ambient scope:** ambient motion appears only on the Overview hero (`section.about-hero`) and on project cards (`a.about-project` on Overview, `li.more-project` on Projects). Case studies, Experience, Notes and Contact stay calm.
- **Shared rhythm:**
  - easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`
  - quick `150ms`, normal `250ms`, slow `400ms`
- **Reduce motion:** reduce motion = nothing moves continuously. Ambient effects show a still frame, and transitions are instant or a plain fade. In Motion this comes from `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`; in CSS, from `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rules.
- **Ambient performance:**
  - Effects pause when off screen or when `document.visibilityState === 'hidden'`.
  - No animated `filter: blur()`. Animate `transform` and `opacity` only, or an `@property` angle.
  - No layout shift: float uses `transform` only.
- **Intensity:** quieter than mockup "B, ambient". The glow layer's peak opacity is ≤ `0.35`, and the slowest loop is ≥ `20s`.
- **What must keep working:** no scroll hijacking, and no change to the demos in `src/components/explorer/`. Do not edit `src/vcard.css`. These must keep working: `goTo`, "Ask about this", "Play the example run", Escape / Ctrl+`, and focus returning to the opener.
- **Plan decision (spec allows the builder to choose):**
  - **Tab switches:** all tabs stay mounted. The incoming page rises 8px and fades in over normal (250ms), and the outgoing page hides at once, matching the approved before/after mockup. This keeps demo state, `goTo` scrolling and the `h1` focus move unchanged.

## Review Focus

1. **A closed console must be truly gone.** After the slide-down finishes, the console must be invisible, unreachable by Tab, and hidden from screen readers. During the slide it must not trap clicks. (Task 6 browser check.)
2. **Fast or repeated navigation.** Clicking tabs quickly, or using `goTo` / "Play the example run" during an entrance animation, must still land on the anchor with the page `h1` focused. (Task 5 browser check.)
3. **Reduce motion.** With reduce motion on, nothing loops: no glow drift, shimmer, border light or float. The nav highlight and console change instantly or by fade. (Tasks 3, 4 and 6 CSS rules, plus the Task 1 `MotionConfig`.)
4. **Ambient effects stop when nobody can see them.** Off screen, or with the browser tab hidden, they stop running. (Task 2 unit test, plus the Task 3 browser check.)
5. **Dragging the console resize handle stays instant.** The new height transition must not apply while dragging. (Task 6 browser check.)

- Modify: `src/workspace.css`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `shouldAnimate({ inView: boolean, pageVisible: boolean }) → boolean`
  - `useAmbient(ref)`: keeps `ref.current.dataset.ambient` set to `'on'` or `'off'`, using IntersectionObserver (threshold `0`) and `document` `visibilitychange`. It defaults to `'on'` when IntersectionObserver is missing, and cleans up its observers on unmount.
